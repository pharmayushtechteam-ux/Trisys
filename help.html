How do I create a shipment and schedule a doorstep pickup?

Select your origin country, destination country, service/package category (Document, Parcel, Medicine, E-commerce, or Bulk & Commercial), and shipment weight in the quote widget on the Home page or Get a Quote page. Then click Schedule Pickup (/schedule-pickup) to enter your sender name, contact phone, full pickup street address, preferred date, and pickup time slot.

Which countries do you ship to, and do you handle both domestic pickup and international delivery?

Trisys Express collects shipments from major cities and industrial hubs across India and delivers door-to-door to over 200+ destination countries worldwide, including the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, United Arab Emirates, Australia, Germany, Singapore, France, Saudi Arabia, Netherlands, Italy, and Japan. You can browse supported destination zones in the Countries menu or on the Find Rates page.

What are the typical international delivery time estimates?

Indicative transit times from major Indian export gateways are: Next Flight Out (NFO) in 2–3 business days, Express Air in 3–4 business days (3–5 business days for cross-border e-commerce D2C), and Economy Air in 6–8 business days. Actual delivery times depend on flight availability, origin pickup location, destination postcode, and destination customs inspection.

How do I track my shipment with an AWB number?

Open the Track Shipment page (or press / to open Quick Search) and enter your Airway Bill (AWB) or shipment reference number. Please note that live carrier scan events require an active carrier tracking API connection in the backend; if your AWB requires manual trace assistance, you can submit a tracking enquiry on the Contact Support page.

How are shipping charges and volumetric (chargeable) weight calculated?

International air courier charges are based on the greater of the Actual Gross Weight or the Volumetric Weight. For express courier and e-commerce parcels, volumetric weight is calculated as (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 5000 per parcel. For B2B bulk air cargo, volumetric weight is calculated as (Length × Width × Height in cm × Pallet Qty) ÷ 6000 alongside total cubic volume (CBM). Binding carrier tariffs and fuel surcharges require backend rate-engine integration or a verified quote review.

What pickup and delivery address details are required?

Both origin pickup and destination delivery addresses must include a complete street/building address, city, state/province, postal/ZIP/PIN code, and a reachable local telephone number for the sender and recipient. Carrier networks cannot deliver international courier shipments to P.O. Boxes without a physical street address and recipient contact number.

What documents are required for international customs clearance?

All non-document international shipments require: (1) a government-issued photo ID / KYC document of the sender (Aadhaar, PAN, or Passport for individuals; IEC and GSTIN for businesses), (2) a signed Proforma or Commercial Invoice with itemized descriptions, 6-to-8 digit HS codes, and declared values, and (3) a Packing List for multi-box or commercial shipments. Visit our Customs & Documents page to download official templates.

Can I ship prescription medicines abroad? What paperwork is needed?

Yes. Personal-use prescription medicines (up to a 90-day supply) can be shipped through our Medicine Courier service when accompanied by: (1) a valid doctor's prescription matching the patient/recipient's name, (2) an itemized retail chemist/pharmacy tax invoice showing batch numbers and expiry dates, and (3) sender and recipient photo ID copies. Narcotic, psychotropic, or unapproved controlled substances are strictly prohibited.

What items are restricted or strictly prohibited from international shipping?

Strictly Prohibited: Narcotics and illegal drugs, currency notes, bullion, precious stones/jewellery, firearms/ammunition, fireworks/explosives, flammable liquids/aerosols, loose lithium-metal batteries, counterfeit goods, and live animals/perishables. Restricted (Requires Compliance Review): Prescription medicines, liquids/cosmetics, electronics with contained lithium-ion batteries (UN3481), and industrial machinery parts.

Who pays destination customs duties and import taxes (DDU vs. DDP)?

Under standard DDU / DAP (Delivered Duty Unpaid) shipments, the destination country's customs authority assesses import duty/VAT/GST based on local de-minimis thresholds and collects it from the recipient prior to delivery. Business and e-commerce shippers can also request DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) or IOSS clearance on supported trade corridors so duties are settled upfront.

What should I do if a shipment is delayed in customs, cancelled, or requires a damage/loss claim?

If a shipment is held in customs, our team will notify the sender or consignee of any missing KYC, commercial invoice clarification, or duty payment required by customs. Cancellations can be requested before a shipment is physically handed over to the international linehaul carrier. For transit damage or loss claims, submit a formal report with your AWB reference, photos, and commercial invoice via the Contact Support form.

How do I open a Business Account or apply for Volume Discounts, E-commerce Integrations, and Monthly GST Billing?

Click Business Solutions in the top navigation bar or visit Open Business Account to submit your company details, GSTIN/IEC, and monthly volume profile. You can also submit dedicated requests for Volume Discounts, Shopify & WooCommerce Integrations, a Dedicated Account Manager, or Credit & Monthly Billing. All business applications start in Pending status and are activated after administrator verification.

International Export & Import Compliance

Customs & Shipping Documentation Guide

Every international shipment crossing borders must clear both Indian export customs and the destination country's import customs authority. Accurate documentation prevents holds, re-weighs, and return-to-origin charges.

1. Commercial & Proforma Invoices

Required for all non-document parcels, e-commerce orders, and cargo. Must list full sender & recipient details, itemized goods description (avoid vague terms like "gift" or "samples"), 6-to-8 digit HS codes, country of origin, unit value, currency, and reason for export.

2. Detailed Packing Lists

Mandatory for multi-box consignments and B2B palletized freight. Maps each item and quantity to its specific carton or pallet number along with individual package dimensions (L × W × H cm), net weight, and gross weight.

3. Customs Declarations (CSB-IV / CSB-V / BOE)

Personal gifts and commercial samples ship under Courier Shipping Bill CSB-IV. Cross-border e-commerce exports ship under CSB-V. High-value B2B commercial cargo ships under formal Shipping Bill / Bill of Entry with LUT / Bond and AD Code registration.

4. Sender KYC & Recipient Identification

Indian export regulations require government photo KYC for the sender (Aadhaar, Passport, or PAN for individuals; IEC Certificate, GSTIN, and Company PAN for businesses) plus accurate recipient contact details and tax/EORI ID where required by destination customs.
