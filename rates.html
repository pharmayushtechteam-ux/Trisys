Frequently Asked Questions

How long does international delivery take?

Express Air delivers in 3–4 business days to most countries. Economy takes 6–8 days and Next Flight Out 2–3 days.

How do I track my shipment?

Use the Track Shipment tab on the home page and enter your AWB / tracking number for live updates.

Customs & Documents

  • Government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN or Passport) of the sender
  • Commercial or proforma invoice for parcels with value
  • Prescription & bill for medicine shipments
  • KYC documents for business / bulk accounts
