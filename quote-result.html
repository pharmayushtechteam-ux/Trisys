    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does international delivery take?

    Express Air delivers in 3–4 business days to most countries. Economy takes 6–8 days and Next Flight Out 2–3 days.

    How do I track my shipment?

    Use the Track Shipment tab on the home page and enter your AWB / tracking number for live updates.

    Can I send medicines abroad?

    Yes. Prescription medicines can be shipped with a valid doctor's prescription and purchase bill. Our team helps with customs paperwork.

    Do you offer doorstep pickup?

    Yes, free doorstep pickup is available across major Indian cities. Book a slot from the Schedule Pickup tab.
