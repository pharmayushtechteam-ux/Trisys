Volume-Based Shipping Programme

How Trisys Express Volume Discounts Work

Our corporate volume programme aggregates your monthly international shipments and total chargeable weight across all 200+ destination countries. As your monthly parcel or freight volume grows, your account qualifies for higher commercial brackets.

Tier 1 — Starter Business (1–49 Shipments / Mo)

Designed for emerging exporters and D2C brands. Includes free doorstep pickup across major Indian metro hubs, digital commercial invoice templates, and standard export KYC onboarding.

Tier 2 — Growth Commercial (50–199 Shipments / Mo)

Qualifies for lane-specific commercial rate cards on primary corridors (US, UK, EU, UAE, Australia, Canada), priority export screening, and multi-parcel consolidation.

Tier 3 — High-Volume Corporate (200–499 Shipments / Mo)

Includes custom weight-slab tariffs, dedicated key account manager assignment, automated Shopify/WooCommerce webhook fulfilment, and fortnightly/monthly consolidated billing eligibility.

Tier 4 — Enterprise Custom Contract (500+ Shipments / Mo)

Bespoke annual SLA contracts, custom dimensional-divisor underwriting, charter/priority air-cargo allotment, and direct ERP/WMS API integration.

Integration Setup & Connection Instructions

  1. Submit Store Onboarding Request: Complete the integration setup form below with your store platform (`Shopify`, `WooCommerce`, or `Custom REST API`) and store URL.

Security & Integration Architecture

How Trisys Express protects your store and customer data:

  • No API keys, OAuth tokens, or merchant secrets are ever stored or exposed in browser code

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does international delivery take?

Express Air delivers in 3–4 business days to most countries. Economy takes 6–8 days and Next Flight Out 2–3 days.

How do I track my shipment?

Use the Track Shipment tab on the home page and enter your AWB / tracking number for live updates.

Can I send medicines abroad?

Yes. Prescription medicines can be shipped with a valid doctor's prescription and purchase bill. Our team helps with customs paperwork.

Do you offer doorstep pickup?

Yes, free doorstep pickup is available across major Indian cities. Book a slot from the Schedule Pickup tab.

Customs & Documents

  • Government photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN or Passport) of the sender
  • Commercial or proforma invoice for parcels with value
  • Prescription & bill for medicine shipments
  • KYC documents for business / bulk accounts
